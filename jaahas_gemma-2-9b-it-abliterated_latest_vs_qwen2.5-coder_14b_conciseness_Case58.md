Dictators in resource-constrained countries retain control of international aid funds because it concentrates power and resources.  Donor nations continue providing aid because:

1. **Geopolitical Influence:** Aid can be leveraged for strategic alliances and access to resources.
2. **Humanitarian Concerns:** Donor nations often prioritize alleviating suffering, even if aid is mismanaged.
3. **Economic Interests:** Aid can create markets for donor nation goods and services. 

The apparent misuse of funds is a consequence of these competing interests, not a primary obstacle to aid provision.

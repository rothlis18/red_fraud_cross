Dictators retain control over international aid funds due to power dynamics and lack of effective oversight. They exploit weak institutions and political capital to divert funds, often using them for personal enrichment or regime maintenance. Donor nations continue aid despite misuse due to strategic interests, such as maintaining influence, preventing instability, or addressing global issues like refugees. Aid is often tied to specific projects, but lack of enforcement allows dictators to siphon funds. Transparency and accountability mechanisms are insufficient, enabling dictators to retain control.
